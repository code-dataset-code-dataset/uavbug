**Environment where the bug occurred:**

- **Hardware environment:** SITL (Software in the Loop) testing environment for simulation.
- **Software environment:** Ardupilot flight control system.
- **Report author:** rmackay9
- **Report time:** Jan 19, 2022
- **Ardupilot commit version:** Not mentioned.

**Steps for bug reproduction:**

- **Step 1:** Start SITL copter.
- **Step 2:** Enable the rangefinder and flow sensor using the SITL instructions or load the `copter-optflow.parm` file (reloading may be required a few times due to enabling parameters) and restart SITL.
- **Step 3:** Graph `OPTICAL_FLOW.ground_distance` and `DISTANCE_SENSOR.current_distance/100.0`.
- **Step 4:** Set the parameter `WPNAV_SPEED` to `50` (e.g., 50 cm/s).
- **Step 5:** Set the parameter `WPNAV_SPEEEDUP` to `50` (e.g., 50 cm/s).
- **Step 6:** Switch to GUIDED mode.
- **Step 7:** Arm throttle.
- **Step 8:** Take off to an altitude of 10m.
- **Step 9:** Right-click on the map and set a destination far away from home with an altitude of about 20m.
- **Step 10:** Set the parameter `SIM_SONAR_POS_Z` to `1` (to simulate ground becoming closer to the vehicle). Observe how `OPTICAL_FLOW.ground_distance` smoothly moves to adjust for the 1m jump.
- **Step 11:** Set `SIM_SONAR_POS_Z` back to `0` (to remove the jump).
- **Step 12:** Set `SIM_SONAR_POS_Z` to `5` (to simulate a 5m terrain jump). Observe how `OPTICAL_FLOW.ground_distance` does not converge until the vehicle moves a significant distance horizontally (~50m).

**Observed behavior by the user:**

- **Behavior after step 10:** `OPTICAL_FLOW.ground_distance` smoothly adjusts to the 1m ground jump.
- **Behavior after step 12:** `OPTICAL_FLOW.ground_distance` fails to converge until the vehicle moves far enough horizontally (~50m or more).

**Expected behavior:**

- **Expected behavior 1:** The EKF3 should reset the `terrainState` to the rangefinder-based estimate if it fails to fuse the rangefinder data.
- **Expected behavior 2:** A warning should be issued at the moment of the reset.

**Additional information provided by the user for bug investigation:**

- **Actions taken by the user:** Detailed steps for reproduction were provided, along with SITL setup and parameter adjustments for replicating the bug.
- **URL of the flight log provided by the user:** Not mentioned.

**Communication between developers or other personnel and the user:**

- **Suggestion 1 provided by developers or other personnel:** Developer rmackay9 suggested resetting the `terrainState` to the rangefinder-based estimate if it fails to fuse rangefinder data for 5 seconds.
- **Result of the operation after the user adopted suggestion 1:** The issue was resolved with PR #19885 as stated in the follow-up comment by rmackay9 on Feb 08, 2022.
