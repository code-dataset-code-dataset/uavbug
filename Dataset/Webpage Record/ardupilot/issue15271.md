### Report Content:
Copter's Guided mode handling of DO_REPOSITION calls set_destination twice (code is here).  The intention is to not affect the vehicle's state in case the set_destination fails because the target is unreachable due to fences or because of missing terrain data.  I.e. if the GCS sends a do-reposition message but Guided cannot actually move to the desired location it fails but the vehicle keeps doing whatever it was doing.  
The vehicle's state is affected however by the first set_destination and could cause odd behaviour which can be recreated in SITL by doing the following:  
- start SITL  
- set FENCE_ENABLE to 1  
- create a small square Auto mission  
- arm in Loiter, switch to Auto and raise the throttle to begin the mission  
- after the vehicle has completed the takeoff and is heading towards the first waypoint, execute the DO_REPOSITION command with a position outside the fence.  
- The above command will be rejected but the vehicle will suddenly think it has completed the waypoint and skip forward the next waypoint.
