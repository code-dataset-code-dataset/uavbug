I've found a significant throttle output spike when switching from Auto to Loiter.  To recreate the issue do this:  
- load up a simple mission with a takeoff to 20m  
- arm the vehicle (in Loiter), switch to Auto and raise throttle to begin the mission

Note: the vehicle used had a sonar attached.  This may or may not be part of the cause.
