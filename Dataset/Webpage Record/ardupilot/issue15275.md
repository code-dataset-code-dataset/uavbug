### Title: Copter: PosHold short-term flyaway after large changes in wind speed
### Issue status: Closed

# Report
### Report author: rmackay9
### Report Time: Sep 08, 2020
### Report Content:
This is the result of a user report of a temporary flyaway in PosHold mode after dropping a package that had a significant impact on the vehicle's COG.  
The flyaway can be recreated in SITL by doing the following:  
- arm and takeoff in PosHold  
- param set ANGLE_MAX 2500 <-- limits vehicle lean angle to 25deg  
- param set SIM_WIND_SPD 16 <- vehicle will pitch back to about +24 degrees to fight the wind coming from the South  
- rc 2 1540 <- vehicle will maintain about +25 deg pitch and will fly slightly backwards (into the wind). This is all expected behaviour  
- param set SIM_WIND_SPD 0 <-- the vehicle will maintain the pitch back to +24 degrees and continue flying away to the South until the vehicle is switched to another mode like RTL, Loiter, etc.  
- rc 2 1000 <-- simulates the pilot pushing the pitch stick full forward to stop it from flying away.  The vehicle leans forward at only 1deg so vehicle is barely returning home  
The vehicle can be recovered by:  
- moving the roll and pitch sticks back to the middle and waiting for the braking maneuver to bring the vehicle to a stop  
- switching to any other flight mode (like RTL).  It will also recover if the vehicle is switched back to PosHold mode which resets the wind compensation.  
I'm not fully sure of the cause but it seems related to the update_wind_comp_estimate() only updating when the vehicle is stopped and also the wind compensation is not limited meaning it can at least equal the pilot's input.  
It seems if the wind changes dramatically why the vehicle is not stopped PosHold may struggle to bring the vehicle to a stop.  
We should probably at least limit the wind compensation to 1/2 or 2/3rds of the angle-max to ensure the pilot can always override it.
