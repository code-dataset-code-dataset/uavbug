### Report Content:
It seems that the EKF (I think) can produce a blank pre-arm failure message as shown below.    
We've seen this before and I thought we corrected it but perhaps we only fixed EKF2 and not EKF3.  To reproduce this issue:  
- Upload master Copter to a board (I used a cube black)  
- Reset all parameters to defaults  
- Enable EKF3:    
- AHRS_EKF_TYPE = 3    
- EK2_ENABLE = 0    
- EK3_ENABLE = 1    
- Perform accel and compass calibration  
- Enable a CAN GPS but do not actually connect one:    
- CAN_P1_DRIVER = 1    
- GPS_TYPE = 9
