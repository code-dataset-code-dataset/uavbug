# Report
### Report author: rmackay9
### Report Time: Jun 25, 2020
### Report Content:
I've found that a pre-arm failure message, "PreArm: Compass 2 not Found" appears and is impossible to get rid of without modifying the parameter list directly.  
The issue can be reproduced by doing this:  
- enable UAVCAN    
- CAN_P1_DRIVER = 1    
- GPS_TYPE = 9    
- connect a UAVCAN GPS/compass unit like the Here+ and reboot the autopilot  
- disconnect and reboot the autopilot  
- the pre-arm failuire message, "PreArm: Compass 2 not Found" should appear (twice actually)
